# MSCQR Connector End User Licence Agreement

LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE EXTERNAL PRODUCTION RELEASE

MSCQR grants authorised customer users a limited licence to install and use the MSCQR Connector solely for MSCQR label printing and printer diagnostics.

The connector may communicate with MSCQR cloud services, the local operating system print subsystem, and configured printer transports. Use is subject to the customer's MSCQR agreement and internal access controls.

Users must not tamper with connector trust checks, connector version reporting, printer readiness checks, setup-test-label controls, or physical print confirmation controls.

MSCQR may block outdated connector versions where required for production safety.
