# MSCQR Connector Privacy Policy

LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE EXTERNAL PRODUCTION RELEASE

The MSCQR Connector runs on the workstation connected to a printer. It reports printer readiness, connector version, selected printer identifiers, safe queue status, and print job status to MSCQR.

The connector does not need document contents from unrelated print jobs. Printer diagnostics should include only operational metadata required to determine whether MSCQR labels can be printed safely.

MSCQR uses connector telemetry to support authenticated label printing, troubleshooting, fraud prevention, auditability, and production safety.

Customers should install the connector only on managed workstations approved for MSCQR label production.
